Car asset: generated with the built-in image generation tool, then edited to match Jorge’s white Model 3 reference: white body, black lower stripe, red underline, diagonal white slashes, black wheels and red calipers. Display export preserves aspect ratio.

Fonts: DejaVu Sans and Nimbus Roman. Bitmap resources generated by tools/make_clock_fonts.py with antialias=true in all four Garmin face definitions. Browser uses the same atlases.

The browser is a separate canvas implementation, not Garmin VM or an official simulator. Geometry tests do not prove device runtime compatibility.
